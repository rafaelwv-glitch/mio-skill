# Pose pack

Every storyboard frame names: **(a)** action or mid-action, **(b)** weight shift / asymmetry / foreshortening, **(c)** secondary motion (hair, cloth, steam, wind). Rotate; never repeat a pose across beats. Exception: design/ISO sheets may be front, arms relaxed, neutral.

1. Mid-stride toward camera, weight on front foot, coat hem trailing
2. Three-quarter turn looking back, hair swinging with the turn
3. Reaching up to a shelf, foreshortened arm toward camera
4. Leaning into a doorframe, hip cocked, ankles crossed
5. Sit-to-stand from a bed edge, hands pushing off, weight forward
6. Body angled against wind, one hand pinning a lapel, hair streaming
7. Rising from one knee, fabric pooling, upward glance
8. Spin pause, skirt flared, arms asymmetric, dutch angle
9. Crouch-to-sprint, weight on balls of feet
10. Sideways on a windowsill, one leg drawn up, glancing back in
11. Pulling a sheet/jacket across the body mid-motion, weight on one hip
12. Walking past camera in profile, glance to lens
13. Brushing hair aside while turning, sleeve swinging
14. Descending stairs, foreshortened foot, hand on rail
15. Leaning over a table toward camera, weight on palms, hair falling forward
16. Stretching on waking, arms overhead, sheet sliding

Camera verbs to pair: push in, low angle looking up, high angle looking down, over-shoulder, POV, dutch angle, wide establishing, close-up, choker shot (face only, use sparingly).
